01 / Overview

Abstract

Human players progress through unfamiliar games by connecting individual actions and using earlier experience to decide what to do next. Existing game agents often simplify this process through access to game-state APIs, pauses during inference, or repeated task attempts. We study game playing in the wild, where agents interact with a continuously running game through its graphical user interface and learn from their own play to sustain progress. We propose GamePilot, which places a fixed-weight vision-language model inside an editable harness that controls what the model observes and how its decisions reach the game. A planner coordinates overall progress and delegates local objectives to subagents with shorter contexts for timely responses. The GamePilot Evolver uses execution records to guide harness revisions and admits candidates after offline replay tests while gameplay continues. It credits each executed version by its estimated advantage over the seed harness from the state it inherits. We introduce GamePilot Bench across four games, with Milestone campaigns measuring human-time-weighted gameplay progress and Atomic and Basic tasks assessing general interaction and game knowledge. In one-hour Milestone campaigns with Gemini 3.6 Flash, GamePilot reaches 1.85–2.00× the chapter progress of the strongest baseline. Revising the full harness yields 10.6–11.4 percentage points more progress than revising game knowledge alone. Across three backbones and four games, GamePilot's final harnesses achieve the highest mean expert ratings in 10 of the 12 Atomic settings and all 12 Basic settings.

02 / Approach

Method

GamePilot optimizes game progress per unit of play time under a fixed model. An active GamePilot Harness plays each window while the GamePilot Evolver processes earlier records in the background. The Evolver localizes failures, tests revisions through offline replay, and credits versions after they have played. At a window boundary, it selects from admitted versions using the feedback already available, and play continues from the current game state.
